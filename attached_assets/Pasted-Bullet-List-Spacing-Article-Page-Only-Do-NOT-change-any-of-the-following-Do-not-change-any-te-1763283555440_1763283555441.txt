Bullet & List Spacing (Article Page Only)

Do NOT change any of the following:

Do not change any text, wording, or content.

Do not change fonts, font sizes, colors, or font weights.

Do not change the page layout, widths, alignment, or CTA card design.

Do not change spacing on the blog listing page — only this single article view.

Fix only the vertical spacing around bullet lists to make them consistent and professional throughout the article.

1. Make spacing between bullet items consistent

For every bullet list in the article (under Step 1–Step 7 and any “Use this data to:” or similar sections), ensure:

The vertical gap between one bullet item and the next is small and consistent (around the same as line-height, not big jumps).

All lists use the same item spacing, so no list looks tighter or looser than the others.

If you need a reference, use the Step 7 list as the standard and match all other lists to that spacing.

2. Align spacing before and after each bullet list

For every list in the article:

The space above a list (between the preceding paragraph and the first bullet)
should match the space below the list (between the last bullet and the next paragraph/heading) — or be very close.

Make this margin look lighter than a full section break, but clearly separated from normal paragraph lines.

Use the spacing under “Step 3 – Onboard a small group of 'hero sellers' first” as your visual reference and apply it consistently to all bullet groups.

3. Keep section spacing and hierarchy intact

Do not change the spacing between:

Main step headings (Step 1, Step 2, etc.) and their first paragraph.

The final CTA card and the text above it.

Only adjust:

Margin/padding inside <ul> / <li> lists, and

The top/bottom margin of the list block relative to the text immediately before and after it.

4. Scope

Apply these bullet-spacing adjustments only on blog article pages (single-article template), not on other parts of the site.

Ensure that after your changes, all bullet lists in this article look visually consistent:
same item spacing, same top/bottom spacing, no random extra gaps.
